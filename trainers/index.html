What is a Store Pack?

A Store Pack is a small training file for one location. It can contain your store name, PLU codes, item names, categories, sizes, custom study sets, and photos.

When a cashier imports it, PLUed builds a My Store track around the items you chose. The national produce library stays intact. Your pack adds the local layer: the apple sizes you stock, the seasonal table, or a house code that only your register team uses.

Sets are your drills. Each set you create in the builder — Front Table, Organic Wall, New Hire Week One — arrives in the cashier's app as a named practice collection under your store's name. A cashier can study, practice, or test exactly that group of items, in the order your floor actually works. A pack with three good sets is a training plan, not just a code list.

Good pack, not giant pack: include what a cashier can actually ring at your location. A clean 120-item pack beats a 600-row dump nobody trusts.

Build one in 10 minutes

  1. Open the Pack Builder. Enter the store name. The draft stays in that browser while you work.
  2. Add codes. Enter the PLU, item name, category, and optional size. Add a photo from your device when your store's item needs a specific visual.
  3. Create sets. Group items into useful drills such as Front Table, Organic Wall, Seasonal, or New Hire Week One. Each one becomes a named practice collection in the cashier's app.
  4. Fix any validation notes. The share tools unlock when required fields are clean and codes are usable.
  5. Download the .plupack. Keep it as your editable, offline backup.
  6. Copy the share link or print the QR. If your pack has photos, the builder publishes it automatically so the same link and QR deliver the photos too. Place the QR where new hires train.

Already have a pack? Use Open an existing .plupack to edit. The builder restores its codes, sets, photos, and pack identity.

Bulk import from a spreadsheet

For a longer wall, do not type every row. Choose Import CSV / ZIP in the builder.

ColumnRequired?What goes here
codeYesThe PLU or store code. Keep leading zeroes when your system uses them.
nameYesThe cashier-facing item name.
categoryYesA supported produce category such as fruit or veg.
sizeNoLarge, small, bunch, each, or another useful register distinction.
photoNoThe matching image filename, such as bananas.jpg.

CSV only

Import a CSV when you only need codes and names. The builder accepts common header names and reports rows it cannot use.

ZIP with images

Put one CSV and the referenced image files into a ZIP. The images can be in folders; the builder matches them by filename, downscales them for the pack, and warns you about missing photos without throwing away the row.

.plupack file

Carries codes, sets, and photos in one offline backup. Keep this file so you can edit later.

A pack with photos publishes itself to plued.app when you share it, so the link and QR always carry the full pack — you never hand out a link that silently drops the graphics. A pack keeps the same pack ID when you open the existing .plupack and share it again, so the same printed QR delivers the updated pack. Cashiers who already imported an older version should scan or import again to pull the update.

Photo tip: snap the item on a plain surface — a counter, a sheet of paper, any solid color — and the builder removes the background for you automatically, so your produce floats on the app's tags like the built-in art. Busy backgrounds are fine too: those photos show as a neat rounded photo card instead. No photo editing needed, ever.

Publishing, submissions, and what gets sent

Publishing (automatic for packs with photos). A share link is a short address, so photos can't ride inside it — instead, a pack that contains photos is published to PLUed's hosted pack service when you share it, and the link points there. What's published is exactly the pack: store name, codes, sets, and photos. Anyone with the link can open it, so treat the QR like a handout, not a secret. A pack without photos shares as a self-contained offline link and nothing is uploaded.

Submissions (always your choice). The builder has an unchecked box labeled Submit this pack to the PLUed team to consider for the app. Tick it if you'd like your store's codes or photos considered for the built-in catalog — an optional "who is this from?" note lets us follow up. Nothing is flagged for review unless you tick the box.

Need a hosted pack removed? Email support@plued.app with the store name.

Trainer FAQ

Can I edit a pack later?

Yes. Keep the downloaded .plupack, then choose Open an existing .plupack to edit in the builder. Download a fresh backup after every change.

Do new hires need to pay?

The app is free to start. Level 1 (The Daily Dozen) is free. The full journey, every Store Section and Recipe Set, and full My Store training unlock with a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription.

Does the QR include photos?

Yes. A pack with photos is published automatically when you share it, so its link and QR always deliver the photos. QRs printed from the older code-only format still work for codes; re-share from the builder to get one that carries photos.

Can I use our own item photos?

Yes. Add them row by row or include them in a ZIP with your spreadsheet. Use clear, close photos that look like what reaches your checkout.

Does PLUed work for Spanish-speaking crews?

Yes. The whole app — menus, all 1,087 produce names, concepts, and coaching notes — is available in Spanish (beta). Cashiers switch it themselves from Settings → Language; your Store Pack's codes and photos work the same in either language.
