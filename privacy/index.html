The short version

  • No PLUed account is required.
  • Your answers, mastery, stars, settings, streaks, and imported packs stay on your device.
  • The app has no analytics or advertising tracking SDK.
  • The optional full-access purchase is processed by the app store you installed from (Apple App Store, Google Play, or Amazon Appstore).
  • A Store Pack is uploaded only to make its share link work (packs with photos), or when its builder chooses to submit it to the PLUed team.
  • Finishing the daily PLUzzle sends one anonymous result — puzzle number and guess count, no identifiers — to power the everyone's-guesses chart.

Learning data stays on your device

PLUed stores your study history locally so it can schedule reviews and show progress. That includes answer history, mastery, stars, streaks, streak freezes, region, appearance, and Store Packs you import.

PLUed does not require a name, email address, password, or profile. There is no PLUed cloud account to sync your practice between devices.

Deleting the app may also delete its local learning data, subject to how your device handles backups and app restoration.

The optional purchase

PLUed offers one optional, non-consumable in-app purchase for full training access. The app store you installed PLUed from — the Apple App Store, Google Play, or the Amazon Appstore — processes the transaction. PLUed receives the purchase result needed to unlock or restore access; PLUed does not receive your payment card details.

Store Packs and publishing

A Store Pack without photos shares as a self-contained link or file — nothing is uploaded to PLUed.

A Store Pack with photos is published to the PLUed hosted pack service when its builder shares it. This is what makes the share link and QR able to deliver the photos, and the builder shows a "Publishing" state while it happens. What is published is exactly the pack: store name, codes, sets, and photos.

Separately, the builder has an unchecked submission box. Ticking it flags the pack for the PLUed team to consider for the built-in catalog, with an optional "who is this from?" note. Nothing is flagged for the team unless that box is ticked.

Do not include employee names, schedules, customer information, or other personal information in a Store Pack.

Hosted share links

A hosted Store Pack is stored on PLUed's server so a short link and QR code can deliver its codes and photos. Anyone with the hosted link can view the pack's import page and use the link to import it. Hosted links should be treated as shareable, not private.

Automated photo screening. Before a pack is hosted, its photos are checked by an automated content-safety service (OpenAI's moderation API). Photos are sent only for this check, are not used to train models, and packs with flagged photos are not hosted. Hosted content is additionally screened for child-safety material.

An offline .plupack file stays wherever its builder saves or sends it. A code-only offline link does not upload pack photos.

To request removal of a hosted pack, email us with the store name and any link or pack details you have.

Anonymous daily-puzzle stats

When you finish the daily PLUzzle, the app sends one anonymous result — the puzzle number and how many guesses you used — so the result screen can show how everyone did. No account, device identifier, or location is sent or stored, at most one result is sent per puzzle per device, and the app works normally if the submission fails or is unavailable.

Questions or removal requests

Email support@plued.app. We may update this policy when the app or hosted pack service changes. The effective date at the top shows the current version.
