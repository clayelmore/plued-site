The short version

  • No PLUed account is required.
  • Your answers, mastery, stars, settings, streaks, and imported packs stay on your device.
  • The app has no analytics or advertising tracking SDK.
  • The optional full-access purchase is processed by Apple.
  • A Store Pack is sent to PLUed only when its builder explicitly opts in to sharing.

Learning data stays on your device

PLUed stores your study history locally so it can schedule reviews and show progress. That includes answer history, mastery, stars, streaks, streak freezes, region, appearance, and Store Packs you import.

PLUed does not require a name, email address, password, or profile. There is no PLUed cloud account to sync your practice between devices.

Deleting the app may also delete its local learning data, subject to how your device handles backups and app restoration.

The optional purchase

PLUed offers one optional, non-consumable in-app purchase for full training access. Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. PLUed receives the purchase result needed to unlock or restore access; PLUed does not receive your payment card details.

Store Packs and the explicit opt-in

The Pack Builder can make a Store Pack without sending it to PLUed. The builder includes an unchecked sharing box. Nothing is uploaded unless the person building the pack checks that box.

When the box is checked, the builder sends the pack's store name, codes, sets, and included photos to the PLUed hosted pack service. The builder states that the store name, codes, and photos are sent.

Do not include employee names, schedules, customer information, or other personal information in a Store Pack.

Hosted share links

A hosted Store Pack is stored on PLUed's server so a short link and QR code can deliver its codes and photos. Anyone with the hosted link can view the pack's import page and use the link to import it. Hosted links should be treated as shareable, not private.

An offline .plupack file stays wherever its builder saves or sends it. A code-only offline link does not upload pack photos.

To request removal of a hosted pack, email us with the store name and any link or pack details you have.

Questions or removal requests

Email support@plued.app. We may update this policy when the app or hosted pack service changes. The effective date at the top shows the current version.
