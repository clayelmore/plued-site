What happens after you pay

  1. Your store code appears on this page and in your email. It works right away.
  2. Cashiers install PLUed, tap "Did your store give you a code?", and enter the store code.
  3. Build your pack or send us your spreadsheet. We keep your codes right all year.
  4. Your private store page shows seats used, your pack, and your plan. No account, no password.

Renews yearly. Cancel any time from your store page; the plan runs to the end of the paid year.
